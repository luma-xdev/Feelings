something I never really said

I just like you.

I don't really know why. I just know that I do.

Honestly, I just like you ❤️ Mujhe nahi pata kyun ya kaise, but it is what it is. I just like you.

Mujhe tere saath baatein karna achcha lagta hai. Teri problems sunna bhi achcha lagta hai, aur shayad isi wajah se mujhe tere saath ek ajeeb sa connection feel hota hai. 🥺

Mujhe kuch nahi pata ki ye sab kya hai. Maine toh tujhe hate karne ki bhi koshish ki thi. Seriously, I tried. Lekin main chahti hi nahi thi ki main tujhe nafrat karun. 💔

Maine puri koshish ki ki jo dil mein hai, usse bahar na aane doon. Socha tha feelings ko bas andar hi daba doon, maybe eventually woh khud hi khatam ho jayengi. 🔥

But honestly... I'm completely failing at that.

Mujhe nahi pata mujhe kya karna hai. Kaise karna hai. Seriously, what am I even supposed to do? 🤔

Mujhe bas ek cheez pata hai — I like you or Love you muje kuchh nhi pata

Haan, main tujhe kisi aur ke saath dekh sakti hoon. I can act normal. I can even smile and pretend everything is okay. But if I'm being completely honest... kabhi-kabhi mujhe jealousy hoti hai. 😢 me kisi ko apne sath bandh ke nhi rakhna chahti. dar lagta he muje apni hi feelings se.....😖

Mujhe nahi pata main perfect hoon ya nahi. Shayad nahi hoon. Aur hum dono ke beech differences bhi bahut hain.

Mujhe apni aage ki life ke baare mein bhi kuch khaas nahi pata. What is life and what is not... I literally don't know.

Mera future bhi decided nahi hai. Mujhe ye tak nahi pata ki mujhe exactly kya karna hai. Main bas jahan zindagi le ja rahi hai, wahan chalti ja rahi hoon. ⏳

I'm honestly clueless right now.

Aur aisa nahi hai ki maine khud ko rokne ki koshish nahi ki. Maine bahut roka hai khud ko. Bahut baar socha ki bas... enough.

Tune bhi try kiya. Tune jaan-bujhkar aisi-aisi baatein kahi, shayad isliye ki main tujhse door ho jaun, shayad isliye ki main tujhe galat samajh loon. 😞

But somehow... no matter what you did, I could never bring myself to hate you.

Shayad mujhe khud nahi pata ki mere andar kya chal raha hai. Shayad mujhe iska naam bhi nahi pata. Shayad mujhe iska answer bhi nahi chahiye.

I just wanted to be honest... at least with myself.

I just wanted to get this off my chest because keeping it inside was hurting too much. I don't expect anything from you, I just wanted you to know the truth. 🧸

✦

Maybe I don't need an answer.

Maybe I just needed to say what I feel.
